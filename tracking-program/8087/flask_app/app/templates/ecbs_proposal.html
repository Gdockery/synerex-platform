  • Reduces total non-productive current flow

How ECBS Delivers Savings

What It Is

ECBS optimizes how electricity flows across your entire facility by balancing current across all three phases, reducing kVA losses, eliminating harmonics, and improving overall system efficiency — resulting in lower demand, less waste, and smoother operation.

How It Works

Think of ECBS like balancing water in pipes — when power flows unevenly across phases, it creates turbulence (harmonics) and inefficiency, so ECBS redistributes and smooths the flow across the network, resulting in clean, even, optimized power.

What It's Doing

Behind the scenes, ECBS dynamically balances phase currents, filters harmonics from non-linear loads, improves power factor by aligning voltage and current, and stabilizes voltage — all as a coordinated, network-wide system.

Savings Methodology & Performance Assurance

Bill Analytics Methodology

All projected financial savings and system sizing in this proposal are derived directly from Synerex’s Bill Analytic Report prepared for {{ customer }}. The Bill Analytic evaluates historical utility billing data including:

  • Demand (kW) and energy consumption (kWh)
  • Utility rate structure ($/kW, $/kWh)
  • Billing determinants and cost drivers
Methodology Flow
Utility Bill → Analytics Engine → Engineering Design → Savings Output

All savings values are site-specific, rate-accurate, and directly traceable to the Bill Analytic Report, which serves as the governing document for financial projections.

Savings Components

Primary Drivers

  • Reduction in kW demand from elimination of non-productive current
  • Improved phase current balance across the electrical distribution network
  • Reduction in I²R losses due to lower current flow

Secondary Drivers

  • Reduction in harmonic-related heating and distortion losses
  • Reduction in eddy current losses in transformers, conductors, and magnetic equipment
  • Improved power factor where billing impact applies

System-Level Effect

Because these improvements occur simultaneously across the facility, the total savings impact exceeds what can be achieved by any single-point solution.
